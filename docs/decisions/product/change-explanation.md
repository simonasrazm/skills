# One explanation capability, two effort levels

**Status:** Accepted for publication, 6 October 2026

**Decision maker:** Project owner

Use Explain Change for material handovers: compact by default, deeper when baseline reconstruction or interacting changes warrant it. The owner approved this capability and the compact logical-diff example after reviewing multiple presentation studies.

Meaning precedes representation: identify the actual problem, logical change, context and decisions before choosing views or animation. Each alternative must explain the change before its visual style is compared. Preserve explicit output formats and generic vocabulary; specialized examples and methods belong in references.

Waydriver's previous Delivered/Found/Next handover guidance is replaced for material changes by context, logical diff, consequential recorded decisions/conflicts/supersessions and remaining human actions. Surgical replies stay direct. This supersedes the presentation convention, not the requirement to disclose incomplete work or material limits.

Assurance remains with the existing verification and authority capabilities. Explain Change consumes their findings and shows actionable consequences; it does not approve delivery. Routine evidence stays in the work record unless requested or needed for an action. A deliverable link remains when needed to find or use the result.

The core is small and references load selectively. Deep mode does not mandate a canvas, animation, multiple agents or a fixed set of views. Human comprehension and generalized token savings require outcome evidence; source and structural checks alone cannot establish them.

**Origin:** Owner approval of the researched recommendation on 6 October 2026. The original research informed the design; source attribution is not a runtime instruction.

Publication refinement: choose effort by the meaning the reader must absorb. Volume is a signal, not a threshold. If presentation choice alone is unclear, start compact and expand where meaning would be lost; missing knowledge requires investigation or an explicit boundary. The owner authorized publication of Explain Change and its Waydriver integration. Domain-specific examples remain in references.

**Refinement, 6 October 2026:** Remove the extra methods reference and its loading instruction. Matched Sol 6.1 medium trials compared six tasks with website-backed methods, local methods and core-only guidance. No consistent incremental benefit was found; a core-only historical-status error did not recur in a fresh policy-case repeat. This is bounded evidence, not proof of equal reliability or measured human comprehension. Preserve the core outcome, factual-status, format and action constraints, plus selective examples and catch-up guidance. The owner approved this removal and squashing it into the premature release.

This introduces no additional mandatory reviewer or acceptance stage.

**Delivered-explanation review, 7 October 2026:** Clarify that existing acceptance covers the actual proposed reply or explanatory artifact, rather than only its findings. Rechecks follow changed meaning, emphasis, relationships or required actions; cosmetic edits and verified status/location additions preserve acceptance when those qualities remain unchanged. This introduces no mandatory format or additional polishing stage.

Retrospective evidence: the earlier run reviewed findings and then composed a repetitive reply. The revised process reviewed exact drafts and caught a misleading comparison heading. Two unrelated synthetic tasks completed parent-orchestrated drafting, independent review and unchanged delivery. A reviewer-source omission caused one false finding and was corrected against the original task without mutating the draft. Three historical replies were then reapplied: one required a repetition repair, one received modest edits, and one stayed unchanged. Exact repaired text was accepted before delivery. These bounded observations support the review contract, not autonomous reliability, comparative superiority or speed claims. Reviewer handoffs should retain the complete source so omission is not misdiagnosed as invention. No further global rule or separate polishing skill is justified by this retrospective.

## Present Clearly, 9 October 2026

Rename the capability to Present Clearly and use it for substantive explanations, decisions and comparisons, including when nothing changed. Select content by the artifact's purpose, preserve consequential meaning, expose relationships and remove prose repeated by the main view. Review the whole affected artifact and inspect embedded labels at delivered scale. Keep the existing review-and-repair loop.

Six fresh Sol 6.1 medium runs compared frozen prompts and instruction versions. Selection improved, but drafts still needed duplication and qualification repairs. The repaired example preserved decisions through two revisions and passed rendered review at 800px and 1280px. First-draft timing and token measurements exclude repair and do not establish lower completion cost or universal superiority. Raw diagnostics remain outside the repository.
