# Deep catch-up

Recover the net material change since an explicit baseline: a known version, date, previous handover or the person's last understood state. If that baseline is unavailable, resolve it from accessible records; ask only when the remaining choice materially changes the account. Label partial coverage rather than presenting a selected sample as everything.

Reconcile current requirements and decision records with actual work. Collapse duplicates, reversals and superseded changes; retain lasting consequences and unresolved conflicts. Distinguish decisions from implementation, and local work from delivered changes. A collection of change titles is not a net-change explanation.

Group by the affected responsibility, behavior or outcome. Use consistent names and abstraction levels. Locate the participant and purpose before zooming into a changed relationship. Choose views that answer different necessary questions: context for where, a logical diff or flow for behavior, decision lineage for why. Keep the overview understandable without opening every detail or selecting every change.

Show meaningful decisions with their current status, reason, consequences and links to superseded records. Preserve unresolved actions without repeating routine assurance. Detailed evidence stays available in its existing record; include it only when requested or needed for an action.

Add interaction for useful exploration, not to hide the core story. Animation may connect states or emphasize a causal sequence, but it must not obscure labels, imply unsupported timing, or require watching a loop to understand the result. Respect reduced motion and provide pause for sustained motion. Verify rich presentations in the delivered medium.

Reuse valid records and visual conventions. Deeper effort is spent on reconstructing relationships and selecting what matters, not automatically on longer prose, more agents or custom rendering. Apply the same semantic acceptance as compact mode. Research-derived methods are guidance, not evidence of this explanation's effectiveness.
