# Domain mechanics

Compile domains at runtime from each intent, its operating context, lifecycle,
stakes, dependencies, and acceptance needs. Do not start from a fixed roster.

## Domain Brief

Discover the concerns a real practitioner would need to decide and verify the
intent. For every retained concern record:

- domain/subdomain and why it changes this intent;
- prompt/context evidence versus assumption or missing evidence;
- mechanisms, states, invariants, methods, standards, and measurements that matter;
- common generalist trap, edge/failure cases, and lifecycle consequence;
- authority/source class and freshness or jurisdiction needs;
- affected decision, acceptance check, dependency, and failure severity.

Perform one explicit uniqueness sweep: ask what matters in this exact domain that
the universal axes did not surface. Retain it only when it can change a decision,
test, dependency, risk, or artifact.

## Materiality ledger and specialist topology

Keep every material concern in one ledger. Mark it `ACTIVE` when it changes the
current decision, artifact, competence, dependency, evidence, or acceptance test;
`CONDITIONAL(trigger)` when a named observation would activate work; or
`UNRESOLVED` when routing itself needs evidence. Record an owner and verification
effect for every entry. Never use status labels as a token-budget pruning device,
and never demote a safety, legal, irreversible, or catastrophic concern merely to
make the plan smaller.

Specialists are derived from the materiality ledger. Create a separate specialist when
a materially different competence, authority, evidence regime, professional
artifact, or independent validation duty is required. Merge adjacent concerns
when one qualified practitioner can own them without hiding conflicts or weakening
checks. Every concern must map to an owner and acceptance effect; every expert must
map back to at least one concern. Conditional concerns remain visible in the
relevant charter with their activation trigger and resulting action.

Encode execution dependencies as an acyclic graph. A dependency means one expert
cannot begin or finish without a named upstream artifact; mutual consultation or
reconciliation belongs in interfaces/conflicts, not as two opposing dependency
edges. Reject and repair cycles before dispatch.

The Domain Brief remains available to execution and verification. Recompile an
affected charter whenever a conditional trigger fires or an unresolved concern is
resolved.
