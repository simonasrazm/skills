# Domain mechanics

Compile domains from each intent's context, lifecycle, stakes, dependencies, and
acceptance needs; never start from a fixed roster or an intended agent count.

## Domain graph

Start with the outcome-bearing system, then recursively expose practices that can
change its decisions or acceptance. For each candidate node ask what mechanisms,
methods, evidence authorities, failure modes, lifecycle states, professional
artifacts, and interfaces a qualified practitioner would recognize here. Run the
same inquiry inside each child until further splitting would not change competence,
ownership, evidence, artifact, or independently observable failure.

For every material object, service, or decision, sweep independent seams: functional
performance and fitness; human use and workflow; authority, rights, and permission;
interfaces and dependencies; acquisition and economics; operation, lifecycle,
recovery, and exit; measurement and assurance. These are prompts for discovery, not
mandatory leaves. Retain and split only the seams that can change the current
decision or acceptance; never treat compliance evidence as proof of performance, or
vice versa.

Represent this sweep as a sparse object-by-seam matrix. Disposition every applicable
cell as `ACTIVE`, `CONDITIONAL(trigger)`, `UNRESOLVED`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE(reason)`;
an undispositioned cell blocks discovery closure. Derive leaves from retained cells,
merging cells only after the same competence test used for charters. The matrix is a
coverage instrument, not a fixed domain roster or a requirement to spawn each cell.

When an intent contains a comparison or desired strategic identity, also sweep the
reference class, current landscape, positioning, experience/quality acceptance, and
fit with the decision owner's objectives. Split the practice that discovers what is
currently true from the practice that chooses among it when their evidence, artifact,
or failure can diverge. Claims/disclosure expertise governs what may be asserted; it
cannot substitute for comparative research, quality acceptance, or strategic fit.

Within authority, separate regulated subjects, duty holders, governing authorities,
and approval owners when their evidence or verdict can diverge. Treat the regulated
actor or profession, organization or service, product or artifact, and data or claim
as distinct candidate subjects; shared counsel may coordinate but does not prove
their authority regimes merge. Within acquisition, test needs/selection, contractual
allocation, counterparty viability, and economics as distinct candidate seams. A
coordinator may integrate them, but shared counsel, vendor, or transaction does not
erase different competence or professional artifacts.

Admit a leaf only when one recognized professional practice can own it and return one
primary domain-native artifact without borrowing an adjacent practice's judgment.
Test this by drafting its practitioner identity, method, authority, artifact, and
failure boundary. If the rationale becomes compound, one part can fail under a
different authority or method, or no real practitioner credibly owns the whole,
split it and represent the relationship as an interface.

The resulting leaves are competence units, not mentioned topics. A leaf is material
when omitting or mishandling it can change a decision, check, dependency, risk,
artifact, or escalation. Maintain parent/child and interface edges so cross-cutting
practices remain visible without duplication. Map each cross-cutting leaf to every
affected intent, option, or lifecycle state; do not narrow it to one branch without
evidence. Discovery reaches closure only when every material leaf is briefed, owned
or explicitly unresolved, and connected to its decision and acceptance effect.

## Domain Brief

Discover what a real practitioner must decide and verify. For every retained concern
record: domain/subdomain and decision relevance; prompt/context evidence versus
assumption or missing evidence; mechanisms, states, invariants, methods, standards,
and measurements; generalist trap, edge/failure cases, and lifecycle consequence;
authority/source class with freshness or jurisdiction needs; affected decision,
check, dependency, and failure severity.

Run one uniqueness sweep for domain-specific concerns missed by universal axes.
Retain a concern only if it can change a decision, test, dependency, risk, or artifact.

## Materiality and specialist topology

Keep one ledger. Mark each material concern `ACTIVE` when it changes current work,
`CONDITIONAL(trigger)` when a named observation activates work, or `UNRESOLVED` when
routing needs evidence. Record owner and verification effect. Status never licenses
token-budget pruning; safety, legal, irreversible, or catastrophic concerns cannot
be demoted for plan size.

Derive specialists from the leaves, then optimize topology. Leaf cardinality and
specialist cardinality are deliberately different: independently failing leaves keep
separate questions, evidence, checks, outputs, and verdicts, but may share a charter.
Use another agent when required competence, method, authority, evidence access,
conflict, professional ownership, or independent validation differs—not merely
because two results can fail separately.

Before assigning multiple leaves to one charter, record a positive proof that one
real practitioner in the required context could credibly own all of them; native
methods and evidence regimes are compatible; authority and artifact boundaries stay
clear; leaf-specific failures and verdicts remain inspectable; and the grouping hides
no interface or conflict. A failed or unsupported condition forces separate
charters. For consequential work, cite runtime context supporting the shared
practice, authority, and artifact; a plausible hybrid title or generic professional
overlap is not enough. Topical adjacency, shared stakeholders, or one final answer do
not prove mergeability.

Before dispatch, audit the proposed topology rather than the final prose:

- every material leaf has exactly one accountable creator or explicit local owner;
- every charter's competence rationale covers all and only its assigned leaves;
- every multi-leaf assignment has its proof; duplicate-practice charters are merged
  while overloaded or cross-authority charters are split;
- assurance duties remain independent from creation where judgment matters;
- every leaf reaches a decision, artifact, interface, and acceptance effect.

For a consequential multi-domain recommendation, name the accountable integrator who
owns synthesis and conflict resolution without overwriting specialist boundaries or
granting final acceptance. Charter that role when orchestration itself lacks the
required integrative practice.

For consequential multi-domain work, have an independent context-qualified reviewer
challenge omissions, false merges, duplicate-practice fragmentation, and unnecessary
splits before execution. This is an assurance duty, not another domain leaf. Repair
the graph or charters, not the wording. In verbose mode expose the domain tree,
leaf-to-specialist map, merge proofs, unresolved coverage, and returned artifacts so
the user can inspect which expert views actually ran.

Topology acceptance permits unresolved domain evidence when its owner, acquisition
path, safe boundary, and affected descendants are explicit; block only those paths,
not unrelated dispatch. Topology review does not replace final artifact evaluation,
but one independent assurance role may perform both at separate stages when its
competence covers both; keep the audit objects, criteria, evidence, versions, and
verdicts distinct. Otherwise split the duties. When final semantic acceptance will
be required, reserve that downstream duty before dispatch even though it runs only
against the completed version.

Every concern maps to an owner and acceptance effect; every specialist maps to a
material leaf or independent assurance duty. Put conditional concerns, triggers,
and actions in the relevant charter.

Treat accepted expert artifacts as discovery evidence, not just integration inputs.
Before integration, collect every newly asserted specialist trigger, authority gap,
unowned mechanism, `NEEDS_SPECIALIST` verdict, and conditional whose trigger fired.
Re-run the competence test on each, recursively, until every activated descendant is
owned or explicitly blocks only its affected path. A parent expert naming a missing
specialty does not satisfy it, and a final safe-stop does not erase the leaf that
justified the stop.

Encode true execution dependencies as an acyclic graph: a dependency requires a
named upstream artifact before an expert can begin or finish. Put consultation and
reconciliation in interfaces/conflicts, not opposing edges. Repair cycles before
dispatch. Keep the Domain Brief available to execution and verification, and
recompile affected charters when a condition fires or routing resolves.
